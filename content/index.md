Hi there, and thank you for purchasing **Simple & Elegant**! This documentation walks you through installing the theme, importing the demo, setting up your pages and styling your site. While installing or using the theme, if you need any help, please open a support ticket at [withemes.ticksy.com](https://withemes.ticksy.com/).

Once you purchase Simple & Elegant, you also get **Visual Composer** and **Slider Revolution** for free. If you are not familiar with these plugins, please follow their official documentation:

- [Visual Composer Documentation](https://kb.wpbakery.com/)
- [Slider Revolution Documentation](https://www.sliderrevolution.com/documentation/)

### In this documentation

- [Install Theme](/simple-elegant/install/) — install from the WordPress backend or via FTP, plus common issues.
- [Quick Setup (Demo Import)](/simple-elegant/quick-setup/) — reproduce the demo site, import sliders and assign menus.
- [Setup Pages](/simple-elegant/setup-pages/) — homepage, about/contact, blog, portfolio, shop and social profiles.
- [Site Style & Typography](/simple-elegant/site-style-typography/) — colors, fonts and custom/self-hosted typography.
- [Translate Theme](/simple-elegant/translate-theme/) — translate Simple & Elegant into your language.
- [Support & Updates](/simple-elegant/support-updates/) — get help and update the theme.
