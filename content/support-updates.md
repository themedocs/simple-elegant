### Support

Thank you once again for purchasing Simple & Elegant. If you have any issue while using the theme, you can open a support ticket at our [support desk](https://withemes.ticksy.com/). We’ll review and resolve it shortly.

### Update the theme

To update the theme manually, download the latest version of Simple & Elegant from your [ThemeForest downloads](https://themeforest.net/downloads), then re-upload it following the [Install Theme](/simple-elegant/install/) steps.

If you’d rather update with a single click from the backend, install the **Envato WordPress Toolkit** plugin and follow its instructions to enter your Envato username & API key.
